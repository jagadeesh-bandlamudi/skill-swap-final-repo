Questions

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before your first swap.

What exactly is LearnX / SkillSwap?
A free peer-to-peer platform where you teach a skill you know in exchange for learning one you want — knowledge is the only currency.
How does skill swapping work?
List what you can teach and what you want to learn. We match you with complementary partners, you send a swap request, connect, and meet over chat and video.
Is it really free?
Yes. There are no fees, no subscriptions, and no course prices. You trade time and expertise, not money.
How do I find the right people?
Browse skill categories, search by name or skill, or use your personalized recommendations based on what you want to learn.
What can I teach?
Anything you're good at — programming, design, music, languages, business, photography and more. Add skills with a level and category to your profile.
How do requests, chat and video calls work?
Send a swap request; once accepted you can message in real time, share files and images, and jump into a built-in HD video call with screen sharing.
